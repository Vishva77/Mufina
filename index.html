About the Artist

❖

Welcome to Mufina's Artistry! I am a passionate Henna and Makeup Artist dedicated to creating breathtaking mehendi designs, elegant makeup transformations, and flawless saree pre-pleating for every special occasion.

From traditional bridal mehendi to modern party glam, every service is crafted with care, creativity, and extreme attention to detail. We take immense pride in preparing 100% natural, chemical-free organic henna cones prepared fresh at home with pure essential oils to ensure a deep, rich, long-lasting stain while maintaining skin safety.
